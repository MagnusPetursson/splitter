Advanced Contact & joint settings
Joint placement

Each cut face is divided into contacts between two pieces. Splitter measures room around and behind a joint so holes stay inside the material. Thin walls may need a seam instead of pins; each substitution is shown with its reason.

Pegs go on the piece that doesn’t print on that face. Horizontal holes get teardrop roofs. These geometric checks do not guarantee strength or a physical fit.

    Help

    Load → Fit → Split → Join → Export. Loading opens Fit. The button at the bottom runs the next step: plan cuts, then build and export. Use the step bar to go back; Expert mode shows every setting and keeps your work.

    3D view

    Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Focus the view for arrow-key orbit, Shift+arrows pan, +/− zoom, F to fit and 1–4 to change presentation. Select cuts and parts with the labelled buttons in Split; the numeric offset is an alternative to dragging a cut.

    Joint map

    Arrows select, Shift+arrows move, Alt+arrows pan, A toggles adding, Enter adds at the view centre, Delete removes. Tab always moves to the next control.

    Expert mode

    Expert mode adds an object list (cuts, parts, contacts and joints), one inspector for the selection, and a command search: press Ctrl K. Ctrl Z / Ctrl Shift Z undo and redo plan edits; Esc cancels a drag or clears the selection.

    Geometric checks are not a physical fit guarantee. Print a test coupon and inspect every plate in your slicer.
