Advanced Contact & joint settings
How joints are placed

Each cut face is divided into contacts between two pieces. Splitter measures room around and behind a joint so holes stay inside the material. Thin walls may need a seam instead of pins; substitutions are explained, not silently accepted.

Pegs go on the piece that doesn’t print on that face. Horizontal holes get teardrop roofs. These geometric checks do not guarantee strength or a physical fit.

    A short path to printable parts.

    Load → Fit → Split → Join → Export. Loading advances to Fit. The bottom action splits, then builds and packages the checked plan. You can revisit earlier steps at any time; Expert mode opens the detailed settings without starting over.

    In the 3D view: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Focus the view for arrow-key orbit, Shift+arrows pan, +/− zoom, F to fit and 1–4 to change presentation. Select cuts and parts with the labelled buttons in Split; the numeric offset is an alternative to dragging a cut.

    In the joint editor: arrows select, Shift+arrows move, Alt+arrows pan, A toggles adding, Enter adds at the view centre, Delete removes. Tab always moves to the next control.

    Geometric checks are not a physical fit guarantee. Print a test coupon and inspect every plate in your slicer.
